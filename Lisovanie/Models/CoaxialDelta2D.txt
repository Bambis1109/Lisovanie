# AI Context: CoaxialDelta2D.cs (Kinematic Engine)

## Overview
This class is the mathematical and logical core for controlling a 2D Coaxial Delta Robot. It abstracts the complex inverse and forward kinematics, providing a simple Cartesian (X,Y) and Polar (R, Phi) interface for the rest of the application (`CManipulator`).

## Terminology
- **TCP (Tool Center Point):** The joint point where the two long arms connect. This is the mechanical reference point for the robot's physical limits (`MinTCP`, `MaxTCP`).
- **GCP (Gripper Center Point):** The actual point of operation (the center of the gripper jaws). It lies on the extended axis from the robot's center through the TCP.

## Core Responsibilities
1. **Kinematics & GCP Compensation (Transparent Mode):**
   - **Inverse Kinematics (`MoveToPolar`, `MoveToXY`):** Translates desired real-world GCP coordinates into specific motor angles. It automatically subtracts `_offsetGCP` from the target radius to find the required TCP position, then uses the Law of Cosines (`CalculateAlphaFromR`) to determine the arm separation angle (`Alpha`).
   - **Forward Kinematics (`UpdatePositions`):** Reads actual gear positions from the physical EPOS4 motors, calculates the TCP position, and then adds `_offsetGCP`. The resulting `CurrentR`, `CurrentX`, and `CurrentY` properties reflect the GCP (Gripper) position, not the TCP.
   - **Mechanical Limits:** Safety limits (`_minTCP` and `_maxTCP`) are private and strictly enforced against the calculated TCP, ensuring the mechanics never over-extend or collide, regardless of the gripper's length.
2. **Coordinate System:**
   - The origin `[0,0]` is the physical center of the robot's primary axis.
   - **Orientation:** The +Y axis is considered 0° (Forward), and the +X axis is +90° (Right).
   - Motor angles are calculated symmetrically: `MotorDown = Phi + Alpha` and `MotorUp = Phi - Alpha`.
3. **Parameter Management (Refactored):**
   - Owns the `ParametersDelta` object, keeping the physical state decoupled from the PLC logic.
   - Hardware limits and offsets (`_l1`, `_l2`, `_minTCP`, `_maxTCP`, `_offsetGCP`) are encapsulated as private fields and injected via the constructor.
   - Handles saving/loading parameters directly to/from `ParametersDelta.json`.

## Calibration Flow (Two-Stage)
1. **Hardware Calibration (`CalculateAndSetCalibrationOffsets`):**
   - Executed when arms are mechanically aligned to the +Y axis.
   - Calculates `OffsetSystem` (rotates the whole coordinate system so +Y matches the current encoder reading) and `OffsetArm` (compensates for mechanical differences between the two arms).
2. **Software Fine-Tuning ("Orientuj" / `OrientSystem`):**
   - Used after full assembly. The user physically points the tool exactly at +Y.
   - Calculates the software deviation (`CurrentPhi`) and adjusts `OffsetSystem` to perfectly zero out the coordinate system.

## Initialization (Cold Start)
- `CalculateColdStartPositions` is critical. It takes raw encoder counts upon power-up, applies the stored `OffsetSystem` and `OffsetArm`, and calculates the absolute starting position in pulses.
- Crucially, it enforces **asymmetric mechanical boundaries** (+90° for Up arm, +315° for Down arm) to prevent the robot from crossing physical limits or calculating an inverted state during startup.
